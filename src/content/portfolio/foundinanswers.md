## The problem

Passionate founders launch focused, high-craft product websites — often under 10 pages — or strong iOS and Android apps that look great to visitors but earn almost zero non-branded organic discovery. Single-page apps silently serve Soft-404 homepage HTML on `/sitemap.xml` and `/llms.txt`, brand-only `<title>` tags miss buyer queries, and walled-garden App Store listings stay invisible when buyers ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, or Claude what tool to use.

Meanwhile, the traditional alternatives are broken: $3,000/month SEO agency retainers or unreviewed "auto-blog" AI tools that flood a brand's domain with robotic slop and em-dash clichés.

**FoundInAnswers** fixes technical search and AI indexing at the edge, builds authoritative Hub-and-Spoke topic architectures with side-by-side comparison tables, and drips out human-approved guides on a predictable schedule — without ever touching the customer's existing app or publishing a single word without founder sign-off.

## What we ship

- **Step 1 Free Live Edge Audit & Custom Showroom** — Performs a real-time 6-stage HTTP edge inspection of `/`, `/sitemap.xml`, `/robots.txt`, `/llms.txt`, `/blog`, `/compare`, JSON-LD structured data, and Apple App Store / Google Play ASO metadata. Generates a custom Showroom with a 6-Hub, 24-Spoke growth blueprint and production-ready sample guides.
- **100% Honest Audit Guarantee ($0/mo Verdict)** — Zero fabricated urgency or hardcoded 72/100 scare scores. If a domain already passes technical SEO and AI Answer Engine checks, FoundInAnswers awards a 90–100/100 score and recommends **$0/mo (No Plan Needed)**.
- **Two-Gate Founder Governance** — **Gate 1 (Before We Write):** Founders customize proposed Topic Hub and spoke titles and lock in voice, tone, and competitor guardrails. **Gate 2 (Before We Publish):** Every 1,250-word draft enters a built-in WYSIWYG Review & Approval Queue with a strict zero-em-dash editorial filter. Auto-publish is off by default.
- **Mobile App ASO-to-Web Bridge** — Extracts verified Apple App Store and Google Play ratings, reviews, and feature pillars and translates them into open-web comparison matrices, FAQPage JSON-LD schema, and `/llms.txt` manifests that AI assistants can cite.
- **Zero-Touch Technical Delivery Bridge & Instant Indexing** — Deploys cleanly via `guides.yourdomain.com` CNAME, a 1-line edge proxy, or static `/sitemap.xml` and `/llms.txt` feeds, paired with automated Tuesday/Thursday drip releases and instant Microsoft Bing IndexNow pings.

## How we built it

TypeScript and React 18 on Vite with an editorial warm-paper aesthetic (`Merriweather` serif + `Noto Sans`), backed by Cloudflare Workers and D1 (SQLite at the edge). The live audit engine inspects real DOM metadata, XML sitemaps, Soft-404 SPA routing traps, AI crawler rules (`GPTBot`, `OAI-SearchBot`, `PerplexityBot`, `ClaudeBot`, `Google-Extended`), `/llms.txt` catalogs, JSON-LD entity graphs, and live iTunes / Google Play Store metadata. Transparent month-to-month plans run across Starter ($49/mo), Growth ($99/mo), and Scale ($249/mo) tiers.
